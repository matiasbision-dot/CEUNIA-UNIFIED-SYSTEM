# CEUNIA Commercial Evolution v0.4

## Goal
Prioritize the first monetization experiment using a transparent rubric, not to manufacture a favorable claim.

## Candidates
1. AI evaluation + audit observatory
2. Inter-IA orchestration layer
3. Adaptive learning / drift recovery module

## Method
Weighted rubric across buyer pain, MVP speed, measurable ROI, differentiation, repeatability, sales accessibility, delivery ease, and fit with currently demonstrable assets. Then 50,000 Monte Carlo trials perturb each subjective score by ±0.6 on a 1–5 scale.

## Result
The rubric prioritizes the AI evaluation + audit observatory. This is a decision hypothesis based on authored assumptions, not market evidence. The sensitivity rate is conditional on the chosen rubric and perturbation distribution; it is not a real-world probability of commercial success.

## Commercial validation plan
- Interview 10 buyers responsible for production AI workflows.
- Offer a fixed-scope paid diagnostic to at least 3 qualified prospects.
- Track meeting conversion, paid-pilot conversion, delivery hours, gross margin, renewal/expansion.
- Treat no-purchase outcomes as evidence and revise the offer.
- Do not claim uniqueness, revenue, ROI, or predictive advantage without independent evidence.

Pricing ranges in the report are unvalidated hypotheses, not price recommendations or observed market quotes.
