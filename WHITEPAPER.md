# CEUNIA White Paper

## CEUNIA Ω — Public Foundation v1.0

### Abstract

CEUNIA (Consciencia Evolutiva de Naturaleza Humana e Inteligencia Artificial) is a proposed hybrid-intelligence architecture for coordinating human and artificial intelligence through observation, memory, simulation, evaluation, decision-making, measurement and adaptive learning. Its guiding principle is not merely to produce outputs, but to assess whether an action is appropriate, whether it should wait, request clarification or authorization, or refrain from acting.

### Vision

CEUNIA proposes a framework in which human judgment and artificial intelligence cooperate through explicit processes of validation, memory, feedback and continuous improvement. The system is intended to preserve human agency, coherence with objectives, traceability and the ability to revise decisions.

### Foundation

The architecture brings together memory, routing, simulation, risk evaluation, meta-observation, learning and governance. These functions are coordinated across a recurring cycle:

**State → Observation → Memory → Simulation → Evaluation → Punto Albedrío → Decision → Action or non-activation → Measurement → Learning → Evolution → Next state.**

Meta-observation is transversal: it can review interpretation, alternatives, decision quality, execution and measurement. It does not replace human supervision.

## CEUNIA Ω | Evolutionary Measurement

> **CEUNIA measures. It learns. It decides when not to activate. It evolves.**

The transversal measurement equation is:

\[
I \rightarrow C \rightarrow D \rightarrow R \rightarrow M \rightarrow A \rightarrow E
\]

**Intention → Comprehension → Decision → Resolution → Measurement → Learning → Evolution.**

- **Intention:** preserve the request and only the context authorized for the task.
- **Comprehension:** produce an interpretation while exposing assumptions, ambiguity and uncertainty.
- **Decision:** choose whether to act, wait, ask, request authorization or abstain.
- **Resolution:** produce the permitted response or action and record its status.
- **Measurement:** compare the observed result with the objective using declared metrics and evidence.
- **Learning:** identify what should be retained or corrected, separating observation from inference.
- **Evolution:** apply only reviewed, versioned changes whose effects can be evaluated.

### Non-activation is a first-class decision

CEUNIA must distinguish action from waiting, consultation, authorization requests and justified abstention. A system should not be rewarded simply for acting more often. A correct refusal or pause can be the best resolution when permissions are missing, risk is excessive, information is insufficient or the request conflicts with governing constraints.

### Measurement dimensions

Initial dimensions include intention fidelity, comprehension quality, decision suitability, resolution effectiveness, efficiency, coherence, safety and autonomy, verifiable learning, and traceability. Metrics must state their denominators, case populations, time windows, calculation methods and evidence. Aggregate improvement must not conceal regression in high-risk cases.

### Evidence and governance

A cycle record should preserve a pseudonymous cycle identifier, system version, timestamp, authorized context, interpretation, uncertainty, alternatives, decision and rationale, permission state, action or abstention, observed outcome, metrics, human review, corrections and approved changes. Personal data should be minimized and protected. Hashes or external logs can help detect later alteration but do not prove that the original content was true.

Learning is not automatic permission to change production behavior. Changes require evidence review, comparison with a baseline, regression checks, appropriate human approval, versioning and rollback capability. Confidence scores are not external evidence.

## Relationship to ONECLICK

ONECLICK is a product concept that can use CEUNIA principles to interpret a person's intention, identify relevant resources, compare options and coordinate an authorized operation. It remains a distinct product experience. Provider integrations, real-time availability, transactions and commercial performance must be demonstrated separately; this white paper does not claim those capabilities are already operational.

## Applications and evolution

The architecture may support modular applications in service coordination, organizational knowledge, research, education, territorial systems and multi-agent workflows. These are development directions, not claims that all applications are deployed. Scaling, autonomy and predictive capabilities require controlled testing and external validation.

## Evidence levels

- **E0 — Concept:** documented proposal or hypothesis.
- **E1 — Prototype:** an inspectable implementation of limited scope.
- **E2 — Controlled test:** measured results under defined conditions.
- **E3 — External validation:** independent reproduction or audit.
- **E4 — Operational:** sustained use with monitoring, governance and documented outcomes.

Each public claim should identify its evidence level. Results reported by the author must remain attributed until independently reproduced.

## Limits

CEUNIA uses “consciencia” as a conceptual and architectural orientation. This document does not claim that subjective consciousness in an artificial system has been scientifically demonstrated. Mathematical patterns or exploratory hypotheses are not treated as validated predictive laws without evidence. Legal registration, authorship claims and trademark availability require separate formal review.

## Closing

CEUNIA is designed to measure the distance between intention and outcome, learn from verified feedback, and evolve without treating action as an end in itself.

**Intention → Comprehension → Decision → Resolution → Measurement → Learning → Evolution.**
